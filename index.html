Bio

I am a fifth-year PhD student in Computer Science at Boston University, advised by Sofya Raskhodnikova. My research interests lie in theoretical computer science, particularly differential privacy and sublinear algorithms.

I also spent summer 2026 working with Tamalika Mukherjee at the Max Planck Institute for Security and Privacy (MPI-SP).

Previously, I earned my bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Engineering from IIT Gandhinagar and worked as a software engineer at Goldman Sachs in Hyderabad.
